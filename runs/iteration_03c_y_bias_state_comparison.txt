## 5. Mechanistic comparison (why states differ)

### Centers at predict time (keyboard15 / full9 letter rows)

| State | top | mid | bottom |
|-------|-----|-----|--------|
| A, D, E (keyboard15) | ~169 | ~237 | ~305 |
| B (full9 legacy) | ~169 | ~237 | **~373** |
| C (full9 letter-row) | ~169 | ~237 | ~305 |

**T035 letter-row fix** matters for **B→C** (Z at y≈305): legacy bottom center 373 ties Z gaze to **mid** band; letter-row picks **bottom**.

### Fit samples (keyboard15 only)

| Target class | A legacy | D current | E proposed |
|--------------|----------|-----------|------------|
| 9 grid anchors (3×3) | ✓ in band | ✓ nearest-row | ✓ nearest-row |
| 4 interior (`row*_x*`) | ✓ in band | ✓ nearest-row | ✓ nearest-row |
| 2 gaps (`gap_*`) | **excluded** | **included** | **excluded** |

Gap Y positions (~203, ~271) sit between letter rows → nearest-row assigns to **top** / **mid** bias buckets and **dilutes** per-row mean residuals. This is the **only** intentional difference between **A** and **D** on keyboard15 (centers match for graded keys).

### Z/C error modes by state

| State | Z miss pattern | C miss pattern |
|-------|----------------|----------------|
| A T061 | (hit 2/2) | (hit 2/2) |
| D S1–S3 | Alt, A, Shift | F, F, hit |
| D S4 | Enter (+763 dy) | Enter (+103 dy) |
| C I03 full9 | Alt | Space |
| B I01 full9 | Alt | Space |

Letter-row centers did **not** stop C→Space on full9 (I03). On keyboard15, Z/C failures are **adjacent-key** (A, Shift, F) or **catastrophic** (Enter) — not the same as full9 Space-row confusion.
