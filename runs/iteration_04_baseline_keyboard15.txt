====================================================================

Purpose
-------
Confirm, before changing the calibration layout, whether the edge / vertical-compression
error pattern seen in the single n=1 diagnostic is repeatable across sessions. This is the
control distribution that Candidate A (keyboard_full9) and Candidate B (keyboard_wide9)
will be compared against. NO mapping logic changed; layout = keyboard15 for all four runs.

Findings (what is repeatable vs not)
-------------------------------------
1. DOMINANT PROBLEM = cross-session variance, not a fixed bias. key-hit 40/73/80/47%
   (40 pp spread) fails the SC-004 repeatability gate. The mean is dragged down by two
   bad sessions, not by a consistent per-key offset. A layout change alone cannot remove
   this collection-quality component.

2. REPEATABLE, LAYOUT-ATTRIBUTABLE signal = horizontal edge instability. The four least
   reliable keys (P, L, M, A) all sit at or beyond the calibration X-span:
     - top-row anchors span x in [196 .. 1083], but Q/P live at x 69 / 1209
     - home/bottom anchors span x in [292 .. 987], but A/L live at x 176 / 1102
   Predictions for these keys extrapolate horizontally and frequently clip (x=1272) or
   land on a neighbour (Backspace/Enter/N/Q). This is the clearest thing a wider/edge
   layout can fix.

3. Top-row vertical clipping is repeatable: Q (and P when it overshoots) hit the top clip
   bound y=135 because no anchor reaches the true top of the interactive surface.

4. Vertical compression (rows pulled toward centre: top +dy, bottom -dy) is NOT a fixed
   layout property. It is strong only in the two FAILED sessions (8f7e: row1 +37 / home
   +46 / bottom -21 / space -46) and mild-to-absent in the two PASSED sessions (d168,
   68627). => vertical/Space error is largely collection-coupled, a secondary lever.

Implication for the A/B layout experiment
------------------------------------------
- Candidate A (keyboard_full9): anchors snap to real keys at the TRUE left/right edges
  (Shift/Ctrl ... Backspace/Enter) and down to the Ctrl/Space/Enter row + up to the top
  row. Directly targets finding #2 and #3 (edge-X extrapolation + top clip), and widens
  the row-Y model's vertical span as a side effect (no logic change).
- Candidate B (keyboard_wide9): pushes a 3x3 grid wider than the keyboard (screen-clamped)
  to test whether a better-conditioned gaze range improves the PCA4->screen fit. Note:
  when the keyboard already spans most of the screen width, the extra excursion is mostly
  vertical; watch the T033 drift gate (larger saccades).
- Because variance (finding #1) dominates, the decision MUST be made on a 3-run sample per
  candidate, not single runs, and judged on the 3-run MEAN key-hit + the SC-004 spread.
