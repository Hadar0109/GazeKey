## Exactly one sub-lever

**Bind row-Y bias to letter-row geometry, not calibration-target `grid_row` bands.**

### Current behavior (problem)

`compute_row_y_residuals()` groups calibration targets via `calibration_row_groups(targets)` and:

1. Averages residuals per **calibration band** (top/mid/bottom `grid_row`).
2. Sets `row_y_centers` to the mean **anchor** Y in each band.
3. At predict time, picks bias by nearest center to `pred.y`.

This is correct for **`keyboard15`** (all three bands are Q / A / Z letter rows).

It breaks for **edge layouts** where the bottom `grid_row=2` band is **Ctrl / Space / Enter** (y ≈ 373), while benchmark keys **Z, C, B, M** sit on the Z letter row (y ≈ 305).

### Artifact evidence

| Run | Bottom cal band Y | Z/C benchmark Y | Z/C hits | Row-confusion errors |
|-----|-------------------|-----------------|----------|----------------------|
| T061 `keyboard15` | ~305 (Z row) | ~305 | **2/2** | Rare |
| I01 `keyboard_full9` | ~373 (Space row) | ~305 | **0/3** | Interior regression |
| I02 `keyboard_edge15` | ~346 mixed (373+305) | ~305 | **0/3** (C 1/3) | Z→Space/D; C→Space; L→Enter |

I01 improved edge keys (A/P/Space 3/3) with full hull — **best Phase 8 key-hit (9/15)** — but **Z/C collapsed** exactly when bottom bias band left the letter row. I02 added interior anchors on Z row but **still** grouped them into a bottom band dominated by Space/Ctrl → row confusion persisted.

### Proposed single change (for implementation)

In `compute_row_y_residuals` (+ predict assignment in `row_bias.py`):

- Derive **three row Y centers** from **letter-row geometry** (`_letter_keys_by_row` mean Y: Q / A / Z rows).
- Assign each calibration sample to the **nearest letter row** by target `screen_y` (not `grid_row`).
- Compute per-letter-row mean `(pred_y - target_y)` bias from those groups.
- At predict: `_nearest_row_index(pred.y, letter_row_centers)` — unchanged API, new centers.

**Unchanged:** `APPLY_ROW_Y_BIAS=True`, ridge PCA4 fit, auto-α policy, smoothers, layouts, gates, regional offsets.

**Expected:** `keyboard15` behavior preserved (bands ≡ letter rows). `keyboard_full9` bottom bias aligns with Z row → targets Z/C recovery without layout change.

## Sub-levers considered and rejected

| Sub-lever | Verdict | Why |
|-----------|---------|-----|
| **Alpha / regularization policy** | Reject | α 0.3–1.0 across runs does not track benchmark (I02-A: α=1, LOOCV 51 px, 3/15). Symptom of sparse/wide cal, not root cause of Z→Space. |
| **Smoothing / runtime mapping** | Reject | Errors are row-level systematic (Z→Space, L→Enter), not high-frequency noise. FEATURE/GAZE smoother unchanged in all runs. |
| **Regional dx/dy offsets** | Reject (explicit) | dx sign flips across runs; zero-sum across keys (I01 §4.3). |
| **T033 eye_box_h gate** | Defer | 25% block rate on edge15; does not explain 3–6/15 on passed calibrations. |
