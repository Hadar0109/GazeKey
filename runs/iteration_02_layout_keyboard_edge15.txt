| Check | Finding |

| Disconnect persists? | **Yes** — strongly on edge15 |

Failures are `likely_cause: mapping` on all three benchmarks. Not explained by hull, collection count (15/15), or a single bad cal gate on benchmarked runs.

## 6. Error patterns (cross-run)

- **I02-A:** dominant **+dx** (rightward), **−dy** on bottom row (Space −120 px).
- **I02-B:** mixed; L/M/Z confused with **Enter** (↵); Z → D (+133 dx).
- **I02-C:** several keys drift to **Space** (C, B, M); Q/A → **Shift**.

No stable regional offset sign across runs — **not** a fixed bias patch target. Row confusion (Z→Space/D, L→Enter, A→Z/S) suggests **row-Y band assignment** may mismatch when bottom band anchors are Ctrl/Space/Enter while row-bias groups also include letter-row interior targets (**fit/layout interaction**).
