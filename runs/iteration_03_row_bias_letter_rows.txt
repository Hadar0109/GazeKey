## 7. T036 verdict

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| Did letter-row row-Y bias improve vs I01 best? | **No** — 5/15 and 4/15 vs **9/15**; aggregate **30%** vs I01 **46.7%** |
| Z/C recovery? | **No** — **0/4**; C still confuses with **Space** |
| vs T061? | **Regression** on key-hit mean (30% vs 40%); Z/C worse than T061 **2/2** |
| Was row-bias root cause sufficient? | **Insufficient alone** — error patterns partially overlap I01; A/P gains from I01 **reversed** |
| Keep T035 code? | **Yes for `keyboard15`** (no behavioral regression proven — sanity not run). **Do not** treat full9+T035 as production-ready |

**Conclusion**: Binding row-Y bias to letter rows was **correct in theory** (fixes predict-band assignment at y≈305) but **did not translate** to benchmark Z/C recovery or I01-level key-hit. Remaining errors mix **row confusion** (C→Space, D→Space), **edge dx** (A, P, M), and **sparse 9-anchor fit** (α 0.3–0.5). LOOCV–benchmark disconnect unchanged.
