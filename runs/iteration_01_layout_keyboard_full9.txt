## 4. Error character: systematic vs random

### 4.1 Session variance (AR-7)

| Metric | Spread across I01 (3 runs) | T061 (2 runs) |
|--------|---------------------------|---------------|
| Key-hit | **6–9** (3-key swing) | 6–6 (0) |
| Row | 67–73% (6 pp) | 53–60% (7 pp) |
| Median err | 63–82 px (19 px) | 53–58 px (5 px) |

Iteration headline metrics are **more variable** than T061, especially key-hit and median error. Which keys fail still swaps between runs (e.g. L hit in I01-A only; Z/C fail in all three).

### 4.2 dx/dy on failed keys (all iteration runs)

`dx` / `dy` = predicted gaze minus target key center (px). Positive dx = prediction right of target.

**Systematic clusters** (same sign/magnitude when key fails):

| Key / region | Runs missed | dx pattern | dy pattern | Interpretation |
|--------------|-------------|------------|------------|----------------|
| **T** (top) | 2/3 | **+97 to +102** | mixed (−24 to +10) | Stable **rightward** overshoot |
| **G** (home) | 2/3 | **+99 to +103** | +4 to +26 | Stable **rightward** overshoot |
| **D** (home) | 2/3 | **+75 to +89** | +49 to +52 | Stable **rightward** + downward |
| **L** (right home) | 2/3 | **−69 to −84** | mixed | Stable **leftward** undershoot on right-edge home |
| **M** (right bottom) | 3/3 | **−31 to −60** | mixed | Mostly **leftward** |

**High-variance / run-dependent** (not stable correction target):

| Key | dx across runs when missed | Notes |
|-----|---------------------------|-------|
| **E** | +9.7, +60.4, **−51.3** | **Sign flips** — not a fixed regional offset |
| **Z** | −16.8, +58.8, **−76.2** | Interior; was 2/2 in T061 |
| **C** | +52.9, +112.8, +8.0 | Magnitude varies 8–113 px |
| **Q** | −40.9 (1 miss only) | Only failed in I01-A |

**dy summary**: Mixed signs across runs and keys (e.g. B: −19, +27, −40). No evidence of a single global Y-scale error. Row confusion remains present but is not the dominant new signal.

### 4.3 Are left/right regional offsets justified?

**No — not as a single next lever.**

| Proposed fix | Would help | Would hurt |
|--------------|------------|------------|
| Global shift right (+dx) | T, G, D misses | L, M misses (already left of target) |
| Global shift left (−dx) | L, M misses | T, G, D misses |
| Left-edge-only correction | Unclear — A now 3/3; E dx unstable | P, G, T with +dx |
| Right-edge-only correction | L (−dx) in 2/3 misses | Inconsistent for M; interior Z/C unrelated |

T061 already showed the same tension: left-edge keys (A) and right-edge keys (L, P) can require **opposite** dx corrections, and **sign flips between sessions** on the same key (E, A in T061B vs A). `keyboard_full9` did not produce a cleaner regional pattern — it **moved** failures from hull-exterior keys to interior fit (Z, C) and kept chronic E/B/M.

A regional offset layer would be **zero-sum across keys** unless tied to a feature that varies smoothly with position (which is PCA4 fit territory, not a constant bias).

## 9. Recommended next lever (iteration_02 — do not implement yet)

Per Phase 8 order (`plan.md` §8) and this evidence:

### Primary recommendation: **T035 — PCA4 fit** (one change)

**Rationale**:

1. Coverage/hull objective is **done** — remaining misses are not hull-exterior.
2. **Z, C regression** (were 2/2, now 0/3) points to **under-constrained ridge fit** with 9 anchors, not geometry misalignment.
3. LOOCV remains high (75–85 px); α auto-selected **0.3–0.5** vs T061 **1.0** — mapper struggling with sparse layout.
4. Systematic T/G/D +dx and L/M −dx are **fit/interpolation** symptoms across a wide anchor span, not fixed biases safe to patch with a regional offset.
5. Regional offset would violate “help some, hurt others” and is not supported by stable dx patterns on E/Z/C.

**Candidate T035 scope** (pick one when implementing): α grid behavior with 9-point layouts, row-Y bias tuning for sparse anchors, or ridge feature conditioning — **not** smoothing + row bias + α combined.

### Secondary (before T035 if quick): **T034 — geometry** live AR-5/6 check

One manual pass: confirm `keyboard_full9` dots land on q/p/a/Ctrl/Space/Enter centers on screen. Not suspected as primary cause (hits improved on those keys), but plan defers T034 until a brief live sanity check.

### Not recommended next

| Lever | Why not |
|-------|---------|
| **Another layout change** | Hull fixed; further layout tweaks risk more interior regression unless combined with fit — violates one-change rule |
| **Regional dx/dy correction** | Inconsistent signs; zero-sum across keyboard |
| **T033 collection** | Calibration collects 9/9 reliably; variance looks mapping-level not fixation-level |
| **Revert to `keyboard15` without fit work** | Would restore Z/C but lose A/P/Space gains — fit should be tried first while keeping full9 override |
